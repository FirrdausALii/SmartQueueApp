Integration map

Cross-app connections

Customer ↔ Partner

Booking & Queue — Customer confirms booking & payment → Partner receives booking. Partner updates queue & service progress → Customer live queue & notifications.

Customer ← Partner

Discovery — Partner shop details, services, queue length, occupancy & promotions feed Customer nearby shops & map view.

Admin → Customer

Platform config — Membership tiers, reward campaigns, challenges, lucky draws, push notifications & app settings.

Admin → Partner

Partner governance — Shop onboarding, verification, membership packages, promotions & operational policies.

Customer → Admin

User & transaction data — Accounts, bookings, payments, complaints & support tickets flow into admin management modules.

Partner → Admin

Operations data — Revenue, analytics, staff performance, customer records & service data for reports & fraud detection.

Admin ↔ Customer

Trust & support — Complaints, fraud flags, audit logs & support resolution across the customer lifecycle.
